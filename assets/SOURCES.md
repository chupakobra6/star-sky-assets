# Artwork sources

The Star Sky Mods mark preserves the geometry of the original Starboard logo. The white-on-black version inverts its original colours. Star Sky Mods’ rounded frames, version ribbons, section headers and typography are composed with vector tools. The collection cover backgrounds are ImageGen artwork created for these collections; other brand backgrounds remain vector compositions.

The three ISEKAI covers use frame 0 of the **800 × 450** [original artwork](https://images.steamusercontent.com/ugc/12230229214356412021/59AE4D6DDC7C4D2DF10F81734F88B4BB70BF7631/) from [ISEKAI RPG LEVELING](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3657580708), by **POUPUN**. The source GIF contains 63 frames. Our covers add titles and a frame to the original artwork.

The ISEKAI artwork illustrates the original mod. The translation covers use upstream imagery rather than screenshots of the Russian interface.

Typography is rendered from **Russo One**, distributed under the [SIL Open Font License](https://github.com/google/fonts/blob/main/ofl/russoone/OFL.txt). The font software is not included here; the published assets are rendered PNG images.

Original artwork, game imagery and trademarks retain their owners’ rights.

The Mods and Russian Translations collection covers use project-specific ImageGen landscapes, with the Star Sky Mods mark, rounded frame, Russo One title and, for Russian Translations, a RU flag added by the artwork renderer. Their sources are pinned in the private workspace as `assets/sources/collection-mods-background.png` (SHA-256 `a01884f97e6dfd7307f5814b727cbda8d7f4e243b330a162a5da95244ea4bc70`) and `assets/sources/collection-translations-background.png` (SHA-256 `23b8ede13d7169535d01ec268c918ce848f9595dba7207127149a0c66eaef93e`). Both published PNG covers are 800 × 450 and carry no game version badge. No third-party mod or game artwork was used in these backgrounds.
